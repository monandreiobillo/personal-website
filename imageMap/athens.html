Athens, Greece

Athens is the capital and largest city of Greece, and one of the most historically important cities in the world. It is widely recognized as a cradle of classical civilization, especially in philosophy, politics, and drama. Landmarks such as the Acropolis and the Parthenon connect modern Athens to its ancient past. Today, Athens is a major Mediterranean urban center and the heart of the Attica region. The city combines archaeological sites with contemporary districts, public transit, and a vibrant street culture. Its long historical legacy and active modern identity make Athens both an educational and cultural destination.
