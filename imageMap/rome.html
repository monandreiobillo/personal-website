Rome, Italy

Rome is the capital and largest city of Italy, located in the Lazio region along the Tiber River. Often called the Eternal City, it has been a major center of politics, religion, and culture for more than two millennia. Rome’s historic core contains world-famous sites such as the Colosseum, the Roman Forum, and the Pantheon. The city is also closely tied to the Catholic Church, with Vatican City situated within Rome’s urban boundaries. Modern Rome blends ancient monuments with active neighborhoods, universities, museums, and busy public squares. This mix of deep history and daily city life makes it one of Europe’s most visited and influential capitals.
