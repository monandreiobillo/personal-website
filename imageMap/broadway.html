Broadway, New York City

Broadway refers to the highest commercial level of live theater in the United States, centered in Midtown Manhattan. It is defined by a group of large professional theaters, generally with 500 or more seats, in the Theater District and at Lincoln Center. Broadway productions are known for combining major creative talent, strong staging, and wide audience appeal. Together with London’s West End, Broadway is often seen as the top tier of English-language theater worldwide. Beyond entertainment, Broadway has shaped global musical culture through landmark shows, iconic songs, and long-running productions. Its influence reaches film, television, touring companies, and school theater programs across many countries.
