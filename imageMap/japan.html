Japan

Japan is an island country in East Asia, stretching along the Pacific coast of the Asian continent. Its geography includes four main islands and thousands of smaller islands, with much of the land being mountainous and forested. The country is known for balancing long-standing traditions with advanced technology and modern infrastructure. Tokyo, the capital, is one of the world’s largest metropolitan areas and a global center for business, design, and media. Across Japan, historic temples, seasonal festivals, and regional cuisines reflect strong local identities. This blend of natural beauty, cultural depth, and innovation gives Japan a unique place in the world.
