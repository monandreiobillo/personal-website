London, United Kingdom

London is the capital and largest city of both England and the United Kingdom. Built along the River Thames, it has been a major settlement for nearly two thousand years, from Roman Londinium to a modern global metropolis. London is a leading center for finance, government, education, arts, and international culture. Key landmarks include the Palace of Westminster, Tower Bridge, Buckingham Palace, and the British Museum. The city is also known for its diverse communities, extensive public transport network, and world-class theater scene in the West End. Its mix of historic institutions and contemporary innovation makes London one of the most influential cities in the world.
